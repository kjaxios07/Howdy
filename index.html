Can I work more than 48 hours a fortnight on my student visa?
The 48-hour fortnightly cap was removed in July 2023. Student visa (subclass 500) holders can now work unlimited hours while their course is in session.

You still need to maintain your enrolment and attendance requirements. During scheduled course breaks you could always work unlimited hours.
What about my partner? They're on a 500 too.
Your partner has the same unlimited work rights — the removal applies equally to all Student visa holders.
