Q "Can I work more than 48 hours per fortnight on a student visa during semester?"

No. Student visa holders can work a maximum of 48 hours per fortnight during semester. Unlimited during scheduled course breaks. Breaching this can affect your visa.

Source: homeaffairs.gov.au

Q "How do I apply for a Tax File Number in Australia? Do I need one before starting work?"

Apply online at ato.gov.au using your ImmiAccount login. You don't need one before starting but without it, your employer must withhold 47% tax. Takes 28 days to arrive.

Source: ato.gov.au

Q "What is the minimum wage in Australia and am I entitled to it on a student visa?"

Yes. The national minimum wage is $24.10/hr (2024–25). All visa holders working legally are entitled to Fair Work protections. Report underpayment anonymously at fairwork.gov.au.

Source: fairwork.gov.au
