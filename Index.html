How Kip keeps answers honest

Trust isn't a promise — it's a pipeline. Every reply passes through these checks before you see it.

1

Verified knowledge base

Kip answers only from a curated knowledge base built from official sources — Home Affairs, ATO, Fair Work, Services Australia — refreshed automatically.

2

Source check on every reply

Before you see an answer, our server verifies every link against an allowlist of official domains. Anything unverified is stripped out.

3

Real listings, never invented

Ask for "a two-bedroom near Acacia Ridge 4110" and Kip links you straight to live results on realestate.com.au, Domain and Flatmates — it never makes up listings or prices.

4

Privacy by design

Kip refuses messages containing TFNs, passport or card numbers before they ever reach the AI. Nothing you type is stored or logged.

A companion, not a lawyer.

Kip gives general guidance from official sources so you can obey the rules and stay safe. For visa decisions, Kip points you to MARA-registered migration agents. For legal issues, your university's free student legal service. For money decisions, moneysmart.gov.au. You always know where the guidance ends and professional advice begins.
